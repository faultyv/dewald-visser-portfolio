## The brief

Africa Paints, a paint manufacturer trading since 1990, needed its delivery fleet to double as a moving billboard for the brand and its "Platinum Series" product range.

## What I did

Designed the full tarpaulin livery: the "africa paints" hexagon mark, the "The Future...Is In Colour" campaign line and a product shelf for the Platinum Series across the box-body panels, plus a dedicated rear-door layout with the campaign tagline and contact details.

## The reach

Every panel was treated as its own placement, so the branding reads correctly whether the truck is parked, reversing or passing on the road.

## The agency thread

This sat inside my agency and Think Local design-services pipeline - the same thread that also carried Solid Doors and Alif Doors catalogue, brand and print-production work. The work had to be practical, client-facing and ready for real-world use, not just a polished mockup.

## Wider ClickLocal scope

Beyond the fleet work shown here, my contribution included web design, social media marketing and content creation, billboards, paint-bucket branding, product-range creation and holistic branding across the company. The linked company website and catalogue provide current brand context; the gallery shows the supplied fleet and banner work.
