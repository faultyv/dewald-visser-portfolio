## The experience

Designed and developed the digital experience for Sitara Indian Cuisine in Morningside, Durban. The website translates the restaurant menu and brand into a clear experience across desktop and mobile.

## What I built

Structured 290+ dishes, developed WhatsApp ordering and reservation journeys, and created dedicated catering, private dining and gallery experiences. Managed the project through content structuring, testing and Vercel deployment.
